About Me

I am a PhD scholar in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at IIT Delhi, working in theoretical computer science with a focus on algebraic complexity theory and computation over the reals. My primary research concerns the Existential Theory of Reals (ETR) and the BSS (Blum–Shub–Smale) model of real computation, including questions around complexity-theoretic placement, decision procedures, and the structure of algebraic decision problems. I hold an MSc in Computer Science from Banaras Hindu University. Outside of research, I enjoy playing chess and reading fiction.

Research Interests

  • Algebraic Complexity Theory & the BSS Model of Real Computation
  • Existential Theory of Reals (ETR)
  • Complexity Theory and Algorithms
